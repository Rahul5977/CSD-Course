I would like to assign the following papers on Vector Databases to study.
FINGER: Fast approx nearest neighbors:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.11408
Innovation in distance approximation methods. C++ implementation
HARMONY: A Scalable Distributed Vector Database for High-Throughput Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search: 
https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3749167
Discusses partitioning schemes (dimension based, vector based, multi-granularity based) to optimize resource allocation across nodes. Early stopping approach to prune computations. Comparison to open source, FAISS.
Manu: A Cloud Native Vector Database Management System: 
https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.13843
System design paper. Requirements for Vector DB, Design goals, High level design, 

design philosophy of
relaxing transaction complexity in exchange for tunable consistency and fine-grained elasticity, performance enhancements
Future work section is also interesting for those who want to pursue research in this area.
Fast-Convergent Proximity Graphs for Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05975
CS Theory paper, new notion of proximity graphs.
Prepare a presentation summarizing the ideas from all 4 papers, comparing the approaches discussed, and discussing one paper in detail.
